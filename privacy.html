Your data, kept simple

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data I collect through tomharding.dev, why I collect it, and what rights you have. I collect as little as possible, I never sell your data, and I don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Who I am

I'm Tom Harding, a sole trader based in Bromsgrove, Worcestershire, offering web design and website security services. I'm the data controller for any personal data collected through this website. You can contact me about your data at contact@tomharding.dev.

What I collect

When you use the contact form: your name, your email address, the service you're interested in, and your message.

When you email me: your email address, your name if included, and the contents of your email.

When you visit the site: like almost every website, the services that deliver this site automatically process technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request. This is needed to deliver the site securely and protect it from attacks. I don't use this data to identify you.

Why I use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and give you a quote. The legal basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, and my legitimate interest in responding to people who contact me.
  • To provide services if you become a client. The legal basis is performance of a contract.
  • To keep business records such as invoices. The legal basis is complying with legal obligations, including tax law.
  • To keep this website secure and working. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in protecting the site and its visitors.

I won't add you to a mailing list or send you marketing without your clear permission.

Who processes it for me

I use a small number of trusted services to run this site. They only process your data to provide their service to me:

  • Web3Forms delivers contact form submissions to my inbox.
  • Cloudflare provides security, performance and email forwarding for this domain.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website files.
  • Proton Mail is my email provider.
  • Google Fonts serves the fonts used on this site, which means your browser connects to Google's servers and shares your IP address with them.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK, for example in the United States. Where this happens, they rely on recognised safeguards, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or the UK–US data bridge, to protect your data.

How long I keep it

  • Enquiries that don't lead to work: deleted within 12 months.
  • Client records and invoices: kept for six years after our work ends, as required for tax purposes.

Cookies

This website doesn't set any advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. Cloudflare may occasionally set a strictly necessary security cookie to tell real visitors apart from bots. These cookies don't track you and don't need your consent.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data I hold about you
  • Have inaccurate data corrected
  • Have your data deleted
  • Restrict or object to how I use your data
  • Receive your data in a portable format

To use any of these rights, email contact@tomharding.dev. I'll respond within one month.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how I've handled your data, please contact me first so I can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

Changes to this policy

If I change how I handle your data, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
