faq

Common questions

What do I get for £500?

A custom one-page website, designed and built for your business. It works on every device, loads quickly, and is set up with HTTPS and security hardening. You get two rounds of changes before it goes live.

How does payment work?

You pay a 50% deposit before work starts. The rest is split into two equal payments: one after your first round of changes, and one after the second, before your site goes live. On a £500 site, that's £250, then £125, then £125.

Will my website be secure?

Every site I build is hardened against common attacks and tested before launch, and my own site scores A+ for security headers. No honest developer can promise a site is unhackable, though. New vulnerabilities are found all the time, which is why Ongoing Care includes regular updates and patches.
