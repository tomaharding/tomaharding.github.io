A recent project — completed with written permission, delivered with a full written report.

ShamiraHub — Website Health Check

Digital goods storefront  ·  June 2026  ·  Paid project

6 issues found & fixed
Medium iPhone and Safari users couldn't complete checkout — affecting a large share of mobile visitors
Medium Internal development URL left visible in the site's code — accessible to anyone who looked
Low Several API endpoints accessible without any login — no authentication required
Low No rate limiting in place — site was open to automated scraping

Outcome: All six issues fixed. iPhone checkout restored, security hardening deployed via Cloudflare, false positive removal requests submitted to 5 vendors. Full written report delivered to the client.
