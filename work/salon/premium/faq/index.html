Questions & answers

Everything you might want to know before your appointment.

Do I need a patch test?

Yes, for any colour service or lash and brow tint. Pop in at least 48 hours before your appointment. It takes five minutes and is free.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please give us at least 24 hours' notice. Late cancellations or missed appointments may be charged at 50% of the treatment price.

What if I am running late?

Call us as soon as you can. We will always try to fit you in, but we may need to shorten or move your treatment.

Do you do children's haircuts?

Yes, for children under 12. They are welcome on any day we are open.

Where can I park?

There is a pay-and-display car park a short walk away, plus on-street parking nearby.

Is the salon accessible?

We have step-free access, and our basins and chairs can be adjusted. Let us know if you need anything when you book.
