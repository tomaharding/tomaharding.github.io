Everything we do, with a starting price for each. We always confirm a fixed price before any work begins.

Leaks & repairs

From £70

A small drip can waste hundreds of litres a week and quietly damage floors and ceilings. We trace the source, fix it properly and check nearby joints while we are there.

  • Taps, valves and pipe joints
  • Burst and frozen pipes
  • Leak tracing behind walls and floors
  • Same-day help for emergencies

Boiler repairs

From £85

We diagnose the fault first, tell you what it will cost to fix, and only start once you agree. Most common faults are fixed on the first visit.

  • Fault diagnosis on the first visit
  • Fixed price before any repair
  • Common parts carried in the van
  • Advice on repair versus replace

Boiler servicing

From £75

Most manufacturers need a yearly service to keep your warranty valid. A service also catches small problems before they turn into a cold-shower morning.

  • Full safety and efficiency check
  • Flue and pressure checks
  • Written service record
  • Free reminder next year
Book boiler servicing

Central heating

From £90

Cold spots and noisy radiators usually mean sludge or air in the system. We bleed, balance and flush systems, and fit new radiators and smart thermostats.

  • Radiator fitting and replacement
  • Power flushing
  • Thermostatic valves
  • System balancing
Book central heating

  • Tiling and finishing
