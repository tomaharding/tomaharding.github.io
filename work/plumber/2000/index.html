Questions

Frequently asked

Do you charge a call-out fee?

No. You pay for the work, not the visit. We always agree a fixed price before starting any job.

How quickly can you come out?

For emergencies like burst pipes or no heating, we aim to reach you the same day, 7am to 9pm. Other jobs can usually be booked within a few days.

Is the instant price accurate?

It is a guide based on typical jobs. We confirm a fixed price once we have seen the job, and it will not change after you agree to it.

Do you guarantee your work?

Yes. All workmanship comes with a 12-month guarantee. If something we did is not right, we come back and fix it free.

Which areas do you cover?

Homes within about 15 miles of Bromsgrove, including Redditch, Droitwich, Kidderminster, Hagley, Alvechurch and Barnt Green.

How do I pay?

By card, bank transfer or cash once the job is finished. For larger jobs like bathrooms, we agree a simple payment plan upfront.
