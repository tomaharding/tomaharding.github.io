About Fernlow

A small, local plumbing and heating team based in Bromsgrove.

Our story

Local, honest and tidy

Fernlow started with one van and a simple idea: turn up when we say, agree the price first, and leave homes as we found them.

We are still small enough that you speak to the person doing the job, and we would rather fix the cause of a problem than come back for the same one twice.

Most of our work comes from repeat customers and their recommendations, which is exactly how we like it.
