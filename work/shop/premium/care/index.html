Plant care guides

Simple advice to keep your plants healthy. Still stuck? Bring it in and ask us.

Watering without worry

  1. Check the soil with your finger first.
  2. Water when the top 2–3cm feel dry.
  3. Water until it drains from the bottom, then empty the saucer.
  4. Water less in winter, when plants grow more slowly.

Finding the right light

  1. "Bright, indirect" means near a window, out of harsh midday sun.
  2. North-facing rooms suit snake plants and pothos.
  3. Turn plants every few weeks so they grow evenly.

Repotting in spring

  1. Repot when roots poke out of the drainage holes.
  2. Go up just one pot size (2–4cm wider).
  3. Use fresh compost and water well afterwards.
