Our story

A small shop with a lot of plants, opened in 2020.

Since 2020

Grown from a market stall

Ottermead started as a Saturday plant stall on Malvern market and grew into a shop full of green, just off Church Street.

We buy from British growers wherever we can, use peat-free compost and recycled packaging, and work with local makers for our pots, prints and candles.

What we care about

Our promises

Healthy plants

Every plant is checked before it goes on the shelf or in a box.

Peat-free and plastic-light

Peat-free compost, recycled packaging and no plastic pots where we can avoid them.

Honest advice

We will tell you which plant actually suits your room, even if it is the cheaper one.
