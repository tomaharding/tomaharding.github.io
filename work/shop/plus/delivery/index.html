Delivery & returns

How we get your plants to you safely.

Click & collect

Free. Ready within two hours during opening times. We'll email you when it's ready.

Local delivery

£3 within 10 miles of Great Malvern, delivered by us the next working day.

UK delivery

£4.95, or free on orders over £40. Sent on a 48-hour tracked service in recyclable packaging.

What if my plant arrives damaged?

Send us a photo within 48 hours and we'll replace it or refund you in full.

Can I return something?

Pots and gifts can be returned unused within 14 days for a full refund. Plants can only be returned if they arrive damaged.

Do you deliver outside the UK?

Not at the moment. Plants don't travel well across borders.
