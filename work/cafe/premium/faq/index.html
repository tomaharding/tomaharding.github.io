Questions & answers

Everything you might want to know before you visit.

Do I need to book?

Walk-ins are always welcome, but we recommend booking for weekends and groups of four or more.

Are dogs allowed?

Yes. Well-behaved dogs are welcome inside, and we always have water bowls and treats.

Do you cater for allergies?

Yes. Our menu shows vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free dishes, and our team can talk you through allergens for every item.

Is there parking?

There is a pay-and-display car park a two-minute walk away, and on-street parking nearby.

Is the café accessible?

We have step-free access through the main door and an accessible toilet.

Can I hire the café for an event?

Yes. We host birthdays, baby showers and small business events in the evenings. See our private hire page.

Still got a question?

Call us on 01632 960456, we are happy to help.
