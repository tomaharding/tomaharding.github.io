faq

Common questions

Straight answers about prices, payment, timescales and security. Can't find yours? Ask me directly.

What do I get for £300?

A custom one-page website, designed and built for your business. It works on every device, loads quickly, and is set up with HTTPS and security hardening. You get two rounds of changes before it goes live.

What if I need more than one page?

Extra pages are £75 each. For example, a three-page site with Home, Services and Contact would be £450. I'll confirm the exact price in your quote before any work starts.

How does payment work?

You pay a 50% deposit before work starts. The rest is split into two equal payments: one after your first round of changes, and one after the second, before your site goes live. On a £300 site, that's £150, then £75, then £75.

Are domain and hosting included?

They're included in Ongoing Care, along with updates and security patches. If you'd rather manage things yourself, I'll help you set up your own domain and hosting instead.

How long does it take?

Most sites go live within two weeks. The biggest factor is how quickly I get your content, such as text, photos and your logo, and your feedback on each round.

What do I need to provide?

Your logo if you have one, some photos, and the key information about your business: what you do, where you work, prices and opening hours. Don't worry if you don't have everything ready. I can help you work out what's needed.

Will my website be secure?

Every site I build is hardened against common attacks and tested before launch, and my own site scores A+ for security headers. No honest developer can promise a site is unhackable, though. New vulnerabilities are found all the time, which is why Ongoing Care includes regular updates and patches.

I already have a website. Can you help?

Yes. A Website Health Check is a good place to start. I'll check it for common security issues, slow load times and broken links, then give you a plain-English report of what to fix and why.
